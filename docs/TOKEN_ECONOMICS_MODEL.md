# Token Model — Scenario Baseline (Not Issued)

Max supply hypothesis = 1,000,000,000. Allocation totals 100%, consistent with the provisional whitepaper. A three-year indicative contributor pool ceiling could be staged into `epoch_pool` budgets; no emissions are programmed into current source.

**Sensitivity example, not an offer:** Assume 10,000,000 tokens budgeted for an epoch and 200 total qualified contribution-weight units. A contributor with weight 5 would have a 250,000-token *hypothetical share if* a binding future policy funded and approved that epoch. In Genesis, no such allocation exists.

The model should add vesting, claim eligibility, anti-Sybil caps, unlock cliffs, net contributor value, supported chain costs, chain stress tests, treasury reconciliation, and regulatory terms for served jurisdictions before issuance.
