# Marketplace Revenue and Contribution Margin

Current status: sponsor intake form writes leads to a database. **No checkout, escrow, payout, sponsor funding or completed marketplace transaction has been implemented.**

Pricing hypotheses: eligible sponsored bounties 5–10% platform fee; professional contracts 10%; free entry for contributors; optional paid sponsor workspaces; AI compute credits sold separately only when costs can be accounted for.

Illustrative US$500 bounty at 10% fee: gross platform revenue $50; assume $8 payment processing, $6 moderation/QA, $4 inference, $3 support and dispute reserve = **$29 projected contribution margin**. Numbers are assumed, not observed. Real model must account for payout provider costs, refund risk, jurisdiction and claim fraud.

First commercial acceptance gate: one external sponsor, verified contracted scope, real funding and settlement record, accepted deliverable, fee actually received and positive realized contribution margin. An internal portfolio subsidy is a pilot, not external PMF.
