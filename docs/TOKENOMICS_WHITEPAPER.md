# Proofloom — Token Economics Whitepaper (Provisional)

## Status
**Conceptual only.** No mint, issued balance, claim, sale, market maker, or liquidity is confirmed in the Genesis release.

## Vision and utility
Token functionality could support verified contribution epochs, project sponsorship, community governance, developer programs and optional memberships. Basic learning and participation must remain free without purchases.

## Maximum proposed supply
1,000,000,000 units (not issued). Draft allocation:

| Pool | Share | Tokens if fully issued |
|---|---:|---:|
| Community and verified contributors | 45% | 450,000,000 |
| Ecosystem and sponsor development | 15% | 150,000,000 |
| Treasury | 12% | 120,000,000 |
| Liquidity and market development | 10% | 100,000,000 |
| Team and builders | 12% | 120,000,000 |
| Strategic reserve | 6% | 60,000,000 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **1,000,000,000** |

## Epoch reward formula
`reward_i = epoch_pool × qualified_weight_i / sum(qualified_weights)`.
Actual `qualified_weight` must be derived from accepted, reviewable contributions, not raw prompt counts. Early participants may earn off-chain reputation today, **not a present allocation of these hypothetical tokens**.

## Next engineering
Formal policy, supply choice, vesting, authorized treasury, tested contracts, security checks, selected EVM chain (Base or BNB candidate), distribution proofs, Sybil defenses, claim UX and true on-chain records. Community input can inform optional parameters without overriding the product thesis.

## Economic sustainability
Sponsored programs and paid builder work should support ongoing maintenance independent of secondary token price. No purchase, price upside, redemption, exchange listing or distribution is guaranteed.
